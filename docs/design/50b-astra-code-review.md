Nine high-priority findings remain on the merged paths. Customs can collect the wrong amount or clear the wrong cargo. At Isidis, the stockpile reading and the timing of settlement can each give players the wrong winner. The NPC fleet and market-listing paths also undermine the supply contest and its prize.

## High-priority defects

1. **[P1] KC.2 can charge duty twice and seize cargo from a pilot who could pay the fine.** Customs first determines that the pilot can afford the contraband fine. It then charges duty immediately. The later fine settlement checks the reduced balance and can refuse clearance. Resubmission assesses the same crossing again and can select seizure. This occurs whenever the initial balance covers the fine and the duty individually but falls short of their sum. Settle the original finding first and key a single duty assessment to the crossing, deferring its unpaid balance under Q-50-54. [Finding and duty collection](https://github.com/conclavio/rustmush/blob/018a2d4fdb458b801a82943d48b6da8ab5023a15/crates/tacsim/src/space/commands.rs#L5260) · [Second balance check](https://github.com/conclavio/rustmush/blob/018a2d4fdb458b801a82943d48b6da8ab5023a15/crates/tacsim/src/space/picket.rs#L1476) · [Uncollected finding](https://github.com/conclavio/rustmush/blob/018a2d4fdb458b801a82943d48b6da8ab5023a15/crates/tacsim/src/space/customs.rs#L1081)

2. **[P1] CR can grant clearance with unlicensed ore still aboard.** The manifest counts all exact lots. Seizure selects at most 16 matching lots and accepts any positive quantity taken. A valid 51 t cargo in seventeen 3 t lots therefore clears with 3 t remaining. The underlying lot enumeration also truncates before commodity filtering, and exact lots held against a listing are skipped. All-held cargo can leave the pilot unable to obtain a ruling. Keep large seizures resumable, reconcile encumbrances, and grant clearance after the full quantity is accounted for. The CR story exercises one exact lot. [Selection limit](https://github.com/conclavio/rustmush/blob/018a2d4fdb458b801a82943d48b6da8ab5023a15/crates/tacsim/src/space/customs.rs#L848) · [Completion condition](https://github.com/conclavio/rustmush/blob/018a2d4fdb458b801a82943d48b6da8ab5023a15/crates/tacsim/src/space/customs.rs#L474) · [Clearance](https://github.com/conclavio/rustmush/blob/018a2d4fdb458b801a82943d48b6da8ab5023a15/crates/tacsim/src/space/customs.rs#L1111) · [Held-lot deferral](https://github.com/conclavio/rustmush/blob/018a2d4fdb458b801a82943d48b6da8ab5023a15/docs/design/50-cut-notes.md#L115)

3. **[P1] KC.2 sends a local Ceres return through Kirkwood Customs.** Free-flight autopilot chooses the first interdicted leg on any outbound route whenever the destination has no direct route entry. Ceres has no route to itself. A ship returning from Ceres’ local mining halo can therefore encounter Kirkwood on its way home, incur duty or lose cargo. Automatic link-dead returns use the same function. Limit this rule to shortcuts that actually cross the protected approach. [Fallback leg selection](https://github.com/conclavio/rustmush/blob/018a2d4fdb458b801a82943d48b6da8ab5023a15/crates/tacsim/src/space/commands.rs#L3808) · [Autopilot route assembly](https://github.com/conclavio/rustmush/blob/018a2d4fdb458b801a82943d48b6da8ab5023a15/crates/tacsim/src/space/commands.rs#L3975) · [Q-50-55](https://github.com/conclavio/rustmush/blob/018a2d4fdb458b801a82943d48b6da8ab5023a15/docs/design/50-law-and-the-corridor.md#L1373)

4. **[P1] KC.2 loses track of unattended crossings.** When another carrier already has an active Kirkwood bubble, an unattended ship can be assessed and told it cleared, then join that bubble as awaiting inspection. The assessment never marks its journey leg cleared. Conversely, a server restart can advance an overdue carrier across a fresh Kirkwood waypoint without scheduling the duty at all. The boot path advances the cursor and intentionally skips the live callback. On an ordinary unattended crossing without a backup pilot or an open bubble, the callback also applies only the transit duty. It never checks unlicensed ore for the fine or seizure that a crewed ship would receive. Persist the entire inspection result against the crossing and consult it on promotion and recovery. 5. **[P1] OP.4 strands each fallback NPC hauler that delivers to Isidis.** Delivery clears the trip at Isidis. The house can choose an onward or empty return passage only if it can price both propellant and provisions at the departure port. Isidis has one bid and zero asks.
