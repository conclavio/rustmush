## 1. Why this document exists

Today in `tacsim`, interplanetary travel is strictly a filed autopilot passage (`+ship/undock`, `+ship/order depart`, `CarrierJourney`): a captain files a flight plan at Port A, propellant and stores are debited once at departure (`D-18-46`), the hull stays `Unplaced` with a leg cursor, and it arrives at Port B on a timer.

To support **Expanse-style tactical space combat**, **zero-G asteroid field mining**, **sightseeing expeditions to the outer solar system (Saturn, Uranus, Neptune, Triton)**, and a **Starfield-style 6-DOF docking mini-game** — while working in parallel with the IC ground combat field track ([doc 32](32-ic-combat-field.md)) without causing git conflicts on `arena/*` or `field/*` — this document specifies:
1. What earlier documents already decided and what the tree has today (§2, §3).
2. The owner's rulings (`Q-35-1..6`) and the numbered decisions (`D-35-1..18`) (§4, §5).
3. The two-tier space model: site-anchored and encounter **Local Space Bubbles** on 1:1 wall time (`Q-CF-32`) + **System Free Flight** on the live `WorldTimeline` IC clock (§6).
4. The 1:1 wall-time weapon triad, point-defense, multicrew stations, light-lag sensing, customs encounters, and derelict/salvage lifecycle (§6).
5. Zero-G velocity-matched asteroid field mining integrated with [doc 20](20-surface-mining.md)'s ore goods, `stow.ore`, 4-drone leash, and the Freeport Registry claims engine (30 IC-day claim contest; 48 real-hour derelict salvage contest under [doc 27](27-ic-field-operations-design-and-survey.md) `Q-S15`) (§7).
6. Three `mush-pixel` views (`holotank.rs`, `space_cockpit.rs` + `planet_shader.rs` + `ship_shader.rs`, and out-of-time-loop `docking.rs`) rendered into `Frame`'s packed RGB framebuffer with a closed colour set of at most 256 colours per frame (`D-21-7`, amended by `D-35-15` to permit 4×4 Bayer ordered dithering on planetary and ship shaders) (§8).
7. Canonical acceptance stories (§9), the [doc 35a](35a-space-flight-review.md) disposition of `SF.0`–`SF.20` (§10.1), the seam-first re-plan `SF.R0`–`SF.R8` (§10.2), and the dispositions of `SF.R9`–`SF.R16` (§10.3) and `SF.R17`–`SF.R24` (§10.4).

## 3. What the tree has today (checked at `830f2265`, updated at `f1ea7715`, `f1b2a1e4` and `a76b1662`, and for `SF.R25`)

- **Hulls & sections (`crates/tacsim/content/units/dray.json`, `dray_p.json`, `corvette.json`, `halcyon.json`)**: `Dray`, `Dray-P`, `Corvette`, and `Halcyon` define canonical sections (`hull`, `hold`, `drive` on `Dray`/`Dray-P`/`Corvette`; `hull`, `drive`, `berth_*` on `Halcyon`), facet bands (`front`, `left`, `right`, `rear`, `top`), component crits, and `"heat_max"` / `"heat_dissipation_per_s"` (`dray.json`, `dray_p.json`, `corvette.json`, materializing the `"heat"` cell on `"hull"`). Since `SF.R20`, `corvette.json` authors its drive law (`"class": "patrol_corvette"`, a `flip` law of 500 mg and 12,000 km/s exhaust) and hull drives come from `UnitSpec` and the rocket equation. There is still no `raider_heavy.json` unit file: `tacsim.unit.raider_heavy` is a Rust overlay over `tacsim.unit.corvette` in `custody.rs`, named from `crates/tacsim/content/space/picket_cast.json` (`35d` §6.4).
- **Standing-order channels (`crates/mush-sim/`)**: `mush-sim` defines `OrderChannel::{Heading, Thrust, Posture}` and multicrew seat bindings (`SpatialState.assignments`). Since `SF.R21`, `+helm/heading`, `+helm/thrust`, and `+helm/emcon` record their orders on `mush-sim`'s `StandingOrderBook` (`mush_sim::tick::record_standing_order`; the private map is deleted), and the space tick reads the Posture channel and the Thrust channel at or below zero; the handlers still apply `BubbleInputCommand`s themselves and then call `submit_bubble_input`, so heading and positive thrust are still the handler's (`35d` §4).
- **Space persistence (`crates/tacsim/src/space/tick.rs`)**: per-entity keys `tacsim:space:bubble:{domain}`, `tacsim:space:flight:{hull}`, `tacsim:space:derelict:{hull}`, `tacsim:space:docking:{hull}`, `tacsim:space:ledger:{hull}` (`SF.R21`) and `tacsim:space:custody:{domain}` (RS.8), each with an identity rule and quarantine on a bad decode, plus the auxiliary `tacsim:space:state` (`SPACE_STATE_KEY`) carrying the rest of the book, the salvage book and the standing-order book. `persist_space_state` clones and diffs on every tick and command; the auxiliary document is unbounded (`35d` §6.1).
- **Docking kinematics (`crates/mush-pixel/src/docking.rs`)**: one 60 Hz integrator, `mush_pixel::docking::step_docking_kinematics_60fps`, used by the server, the TUI and the web (through `mush-pixel-wasm`); the server solvers are `#[cfg(test)]` and no player key runs the auto-solver (`SF.R24`).
- **Orbital clusters (`crates/tacsim/src/pack.rs`, `crates/tacsim/content/lane/ceres_phobos.json`)**: four `OrbitalClusterDef`s in `LocationDef::clusters` on the lane location `kirkwood_depot`, validated at pack load; the Rust cluster enum is gone (`SF.R22`). They are not doc 20 §17 field-kind sites, and a world with no applied clusters falls back to the compiled-in lane (`35d` §6.6).
- **Passage & interception seams (`crates/tacsim/src/port/passage.rs`, `voyage.rs`, `mush-sim/src/journey.rs`)**: `plan_passage` and `plan_passage_from` plan body-to-body flip-and-burn transits with a five-pass intercept solver. `LegDef.interdiction`, `PromotionTrigger::Interception`, `JourneyState::Promoted` (every `Promoted` journey pauses its leg timer in `mush-sim/src/journey.rs:1292–1304` until `resume_carrier_journey`, `fail_carrier_journey`, or `dock_carrier_at_destination`), and `MAX_ACTIVE_DOMAINS` (8, enforced on `ActivationBook`) materialize and stand down domain-keyed `LocalSpaceBubble`s (`SpaceBubbleBook::by_domain`).
- **Sky (`crates/tacsim/src/sky.rs`, `crates/tacsim/content/sky/fetch_elements.py`, `outer_bodies.json`)**: `Sky::Kepler` computes `PosKm` (whole km in 3D ecliptic J2000) and `Sky::velocity_mps(body, ic_t) -> [i32; 3]` for inner, mid, and outer-system bodies including Uranus, Neptune, Triton, the Galilean moons (`io`, `europa`, `ganymede`, `callisto`), and the four orbital asteroid cluster reference anchors (`ceres_halo`, `kirkwood_gap_3_1` at 2.50 AU, `pallas_inclined_belt`, `saturn_b_ring_edge`), loaded from the signed `tacsim.sky.outer_bodies@1` pack (`load_pack`), folded into `Sky::revision()`, and validated through `BodyDef`.

## 4. Owner survey & rulings (2026-11-01)

### Q-35-1 — Kernel unification vs. parallel worktree isolation
- **Ruling (owner, 2026-11-01):** **Unified data & damage kernel, isolated 3D space kinematics (`crates/tacsim/src/space/`).**
  - Reuse the existing hull/section/facet/component/heat/party/custody types (`dray.json`'s `hull`/`hold`/`drive` sections and `front`/`left`/`right`/`rear`/`top` facets, `PartyId` relations, `custody::check_out`, deterministic input tape, snapshots, `WorldTimeline` outage freeze, and `MindRunner` Rhai host).
  - Run 3D continuous kilometer-scale space kinematics, orbital vector integration, and torpedo/railgun/laser ballistics in `crates/tacsim/src/space/*` so zero files collide with the ongoing `arena/*` and `field/*` refactoring in [doc 32](32-ic-combat-field.md).

### Q-35-2 — Tier-1 System Free Flight vs. site-attached bubbles first
- **Ruling (owner, 2026-11-01):** **Keep full 3D System Free Flight (`+helm/decouple` + outer planets) with live propellant debit and return-reserve checks, AND stage the cut order so site-anchored space bubbles (Kirkwood, Pallas, Saturn Rings) land first before mid-leg decoupling.**
  - Supersedes `D-14-5` (node-only orbital regions) and `D-18-46` (frozen passage plan) when a ship enters a promoted `LocalSpaceBubble` or `FlightMode::FreeFlight`.

### Q-35-3 — Asteroid mining integration with Doc 20 & the Freeport Registry
- **Ruling (owner, 2026-11-01):** **Unify both.** Use [doc 20](20-surface-mining.md)'s canonical ore goods (`metal_ore`, `ice_ore`, `carbon_ore`, `strategic_ore`), `stow.ore` stowage (`Dray-P` and hoppers), four ore families with `barren`/`lean`/`fair`/`rich` richness grades, 4-drone leash per `Dray-P` (`ExcursionId`), and Freeport Registry prospecting licences, claim stakes, **30 IC-day** claim contests (`tenure.rs:34`), and Pallas Syndicate paper claims — while playing inside a 3D velocity-matched space bubble around orbital asteroid clusters.

### Q-35-4 — Time base & Point-Defense under "no auto-fire"
- **Ruling (owner, 2026-11-01):**
  1. **Wall-time bubbles (`Q-CF-32`)**: Engaged Local Space Bubbles run on **1:1 wall time** (adaptive step tempo `D-16-2`), while Tier-1 system free flight runs on the runtime `WorldTimeline` IC clock (`dt_ic` from `world_timeline().current().rate`, never hardcoding `24`).
  2. **Automatic Point-Defense toggle permitted**: Full automatic PDC and defensive-laser interception toggles (`+tac/pdc auto` and per-mount `Offense`/`Defense` mode) are explicitly **approved**. Since the game already permits conditional fire doctrines such as `"FIRE ALL WHEN ODDS >= 60"`, an automatic point-defense posture toggle is natural and avoids impossible manual typing races against incoming torpedo salvos.

### Q-35-5 — 6-DOF Starfield-style docking mini-game execution & time loop
- **Ruling (owner, 2026-11-01):** **Run the 60 fps 6-DOF alignment HUD client-side in `mush-pixel` / the gateway, and step the docking hull out of the live time loop while the player plays the mini-game.**
  - Taking the docking ship out of the time loop eliminates SSH latency issues and prevents time-compression drift while the pilot carefully aligns translation, pitch/yaw, roll, and closure rate; when the mini-game finishes, the client submits its bounded RCS control input tape, which the server deterministically replays from the session's initial pose to verify gate satisfaction and merge `Docked { quality, rcs_spent_kg, hull_scrape }` back into the world state (`D-35-14`).
  - Mandatory for unassisted targets (disabled derelicts, ship-to-ship boarding/salvage, unpowered field moorings) and opt-in at ports (saving terminal approach time / RCS reaction mass).

### Q-35-6 — Visual views, Bayer dithering, and cut priority
- **Ruling (owner, 2026-11-01):** **Keep all three views (`holotank.rs`, `space_cockpit.rs` + analytical raycast `planet_shader.rs` + `ship_shader.rs`, and `docking.rs` wireframe collar overlay) within `Frame`'s closed 256-colour per-frame budget (`D-21-7` amended by `D-35-15`), and build visuals early in parallel with backend cuts.**

## 5. Numbered decisions (`D-35-1` – `D-35-19`)

- **D-35-1 (Shared damage kernel, canonical unit definitions, isolated 3D space module):** Space combat reuses the canonical unit definitions (`dray.json`, `dray_p.json`, `corvette.json`, `halcyon.json` in `crates/tacsim/content/units/`), sections (`hull`/`hold`/`drive` on `Dray`/`Dray-P`/`Corvette`; section list on `Halcyon`), facet bands (`front`/`left`/`right`/`rear`/`top`), component crits, heat cells, `PartyId` relations, `custody::check_out` / `check_in`, deterministic input tape, snapshots, W-clock outage freeze, and `MindRunner` host. **There is no second hull definition file**: any space-specific overlay keyed to a canonical `UnitId` may bind only 3D RCS control parameters, space weapon mount bindings, and `pixel_model` visual archetype ids, and **may never redefine** hull mass, hold volume/mass, drive acceleration/ISP, sections, facets, or heat capacity. All 3D kilometer-scale kinematics and space verbs live in `crates/tacsim/src/space/*`. *(As built after `SF.R20`: drives come from `UnitSpec` and the rocket equation, `corvette.json` authors its drive law, the Slow consequence goes through `crate::arena::damage::apply_slow_consequence`, and crits persist through `tacsim.space.subsystems`. Still owed, in `SF.R28`: `SpaceShipState::apply_damage` is a private proportional splitter, PDC and sensor losses are thresholds, Slow is inverted on bubble entry and compounds through `DriveDegraded`, `ceil` survives on the rating, and the raider is a Rust overlay over `tacsim.unit.corvette`, not a unit file; `35d` §6.4.)*
- **D-35-2 (Parallel file boundary):** No cut in this track modifies `crates/tacsim/src/arena/*`, `crates/tacsim/src/field/*`, `crates/tacsim/src/surface/*`, or `crates/tacsim/src/detachment/*` unless a cut explicitly targets that shared seam (`SF.R18`–`SF.R22`).
- **D-35-3 (Two-tier clock rule):** Tier-1 System Free Flight advances on a registered world tick (`reg.tick`) by the live `WorldTimeline` IC delta (`dt_ic`, never hardcoding `24`). Tier-2 Local Space Bubbles advance on **1:1 wall time** (`D-16-2` substeps at most 250 ms) and stamp caller-supplied W-clock IC instants (`Q-CF-32`). **State after `SF.R25`:** `ActiveInterdictionBubble::last_stepped_wall_s: i64` is persisted in whole seconds read from `World::now()` (unix seconds) on a fixed 1 s `TickCadence::Coarse` tick (`SPACE_TICK_PERIOD`); since `SF.R17` each step is `min(elapsed, MAX_BUBBLE_TICK_STEP_MS)` (30 s) with the excess dropped, a backwards clock step freezes the bubble until the wall clock catches up, and since `SF.R25` a W-clock freeze advances `last_stepped_wall_s` so frozen time is not repaid as one step. **Owed (`SF.R29`, `35d` §6.5):** a monotonic source, elapsed time carried over, a sub-second cadence while ordnance flies, paused time excluded, `[tacsim].space_pulse_ms` wired or deleted. No player command may step the world or bubble clock in production.
- **D-35-4 (Outer-system ephemeris & orbital velocity):** `Sky::Kepler` gains `Sky::velocity_mps(body, ic_t) -> [i32; 3]` (central-difference derivative of `position_km`, with continuous `f64` phase on the circular sky) and elements for **Uranus**, **Neptune**, **Triton**, and **Io, Europa, Ganymede, Callisto** (`fetch_elements.py`, using propagated rows post-2250 where Horizons ends, matching Titan), validated through `pack::BodyDef` (`tacsim.sky.outer_bodies@1`) and folded into `Sky::revision()`.
- **D-35-5 (Bubbles at sites first, mid-leg decoupling second):** A `LocalSpaceBubble` (`BubbleId`) attaches first to orbital sites/nodes (Kirkwood Picket approach, Pallas Cluster, Ceres Halo, Saturn Ring Edge) using the existing `LegDef.interdiction` / `PromotionTrigger::Interception` / `JourneyState::Promoted` seam (**every** `Promoted` journey pauses its leg timer in `mush-sim/src/journey.rs:1292–1304` until `resume_carrier_journey`, `fail_carrier_journey`, or `dock_carrier_at_destination`). Mid-leg free-flight decoupling (`+helm/decouple`) lands after site bubbles.
- **D-35-6 (Live propellant debit & return-reserve check):** While in free flight or a bubble, burns debit propellant live from the hull's `stow.propellant` pool in kg. A from-coordinate passage estimator (`plan_passage_from(pos_km, vel_mps, body)` built on `port/passage.rs`) continuously computes the minimum $\Delta v$ required to reach the nearest open port (`RETURN_RESERVE`) and warns (`TANK_RESERVE_WARN`) before a burn would dip below the return margin; `+helm/burn force` is required to burn into stranding reserve.
- **D-35-7 (Offline / linkdead rule in space):** Under `Q-H5` / `P-2`, if all crew on a free-flying ship go linkdead (`SpacePresenceHook` on `Connected`/`Linkdead`/`Disconnected` from real session presence `is_connected && has_live_conns`, never a manual toggle), main thrust cuts to zero (ballistic drift), active point-defense (`pdc_auto`) remains armed for the 120 s reclaim grace window, and if unclaimed inside a safe corridor the hull either holds ballistic with a life-support notice or executes its queued return plan. *(As built: the hook, thrust cut, and 120 s grace window are live; since `SF.R23` the cover includes orbital cluster sessions and the grace expiry runs `execute_linkdead_return_journey`. `SF.R23` built that return as a free hand-built route (`35d` §2 #2); since `SF.R25` it goes through `engage_port_autopilot` (the planner, the tank and locker checks, the debit), trying the queued port and then every open port nearest first, and a hull that can afford none stays where she is. Q-35d-2 was ruled on 2026-11-11 for a mind-driven backup (`35d` §9): since then the grace is 300 s, as in the field, and the queued port is the one she filed for (the port she left, when she filed for a cluster or a body), with the nearest she can afford as the fallback. The backup mind is §11: its space half is built (cut notes, `SF.R32`); the field half and the retrieval job for a hull that holds are planned there.)*
- **D-35-8 (1:1 wall-time weapon triad & automatic PDC toggle):** Weapon ranges, velocities, and flight times are authored in 1:1 wall time (§6.2). `+tac/pdc auto` and per-mount `Offense`/`Defense` laser mode are valid automatic defensive postures (`Q-35-4`). PDC mounts enforce fire-rate cooldowns and per-burst intercept capacity so multi-torpedo salvos can saturate point defense; torpedoes fly 3D proportional navigation while $\Delta v > 0$ and coast ballistically after burnout up to `max_flight_ms`; railgun lead solutions solve in world coordinates (`v_slug_world = v_shooter + v_muzzle_vec`).
- **D-35-9 (Multicrew channels & seats):** `+helm`, `+tac`, and `+mine` are `authoritative` verbs that require verified physical presence aboard the hull and seat authority (`helm`, `tactical`, `ew`, `engineering`, `flight_ops`, `command`). A solo pilot occupying command operates all channels directly. *(As built after `SF.R21`: propellant debits, contraband seizure, and mining tenure/site-work submit via `Ctx::submit_authoritative`; the in-bubble `+helm` and `+tac` switches also submit `tacsim_space_bubble_input` (`submit_bubble_input`), and `StandingOrder`s are recorded on `mush-sim`'s `StandingOrderBook`. The label is still a relabel (`35d` §4): every bubble verb changes state in its handler, the submission writes an audit row to `tacsim.space_bubble_tape`, re-checks nothing, and is skipped when the actor already has an action pending; `+helm/dock`, `+helm/tow`, `+tac/hail` and `+mine/ping|laser` submit nothing; the tick reads the Posture channel and Thrust at or below zero only. Owed in `SF.R26`.)*
- **D-35-10 (Light-lag sensing & EMCON):** System-scale detection routes through `mush-propagation` and compares target IR output (passive hull floor + radiator heat + torch thrust bloom + weapon/mining laser flash) against distance squared and observer sensor aperture. Undetected contacts are never leaked in text or panel feeds. *(As built: since `SF.R23`, `+tac/hail` queues a hail in `pending_light_lag_hails` when its one-way lag reaches one second and the tick delivers it, and `+helm/approach`, `+helm/dock`'s target and gates and the holotank's torpedo target position are gated by detection. Since `SF.R25` the hail's range is measured (a detected contact in a bubble, an open port from free flight or a cluster), never typed, and at most `MAX_PENDING_HAILS_PER_HULL` are in flight per hull; the dock's hostile gate refuses on an operational hostile and names it only when detected. ## 6. Two-Tier Space Architecture & Expanse Combat

### 6.1 Tier 1 (IC Clock) vs. Tier 2 (1:1 Wall-Time Local Space Bubble)

1. **Filed Autopilot (`CarrierJourney`, default for offline trade):**
   - Works as built in [doc 18](18-trade-run-vertical-slice.md). When a leg with `LegDef.interdiction` triggers `PromotionTrigger::Interception` (e.g. passing the Kirkwood Picket or arriving at an orbital asteroid cluster), `JourneyState::Promoted` pauses the leg cursor and a registered world tick (`reg.tick`) materializes the hull into the site's `LocalSpaceBubble` via `custody::check_out`.
2. **Tier 2 — `LocalSpaceBubble` (`crates/tacsim/src/space/bubble.rs`, 1:1 wall time):**
   - Origin $\mathbf{R}_0(t), \mathbf{V}_0(t)$ co-moves with the site body, station, asteroid cluster centre, or interception barycentre.
   - Local position $\mathbf{x}$ in metres (`[i64; 3]`, $\pm 100{,}000\text{ km}$ domain) with micrometre substep remainders (`pos_rem_um: [i64; 3]` so sub-4 m/s motion is never truncated at 250 ms substeps), local velocity $\mathbf{v}$ in $\text{mm/s}$ (`[i64; 3]`).
   - Steps on **1:1 wall time** (`D-16-2` adaptive 5000 / 1000 / 500 ms step with 250 ms substeps), stamps caller-supplied W-clock IC instants (`Q-CF-32`), records a deterministic `BubbleInputTape`, and supports mid-battle `BubbleSnapshot` capture and bit-identical replay.
3. **Tier 1 — System Free Flight (`WorldTimeline` IC clock):**
   - For ships that undock manually (`+helm/undock`) or decouple mid-leg (`+helm/decouple`) to explore the outer solar system or fly off-corridor:
     - Position `pos_m: [i128; 3]` (heliocentric ecliptic J2000 metres), velocity `vel_mmps: [i64; 3]`.
     - Consumes `mush-sim` channels (`Heading{x,y,z}`, `Thrust`, `Emcon{0..=3}`) on each world tick (`dt_ic`), debiting propellant live from `stow.propellant` (`D-35-6`).
     - Supports guidance modes: `Manual`, `Prograde`, `Retrograde`, `Intercept { target, flip_burn }`, and `MatchVelocity { target }` (evaluated in target-relative frame).
     - `+helm/autopilot <port>` invokes `plan_passage_from(pos_km, vel_mps, port)` (`port/passage.rs`) to re-enter a filed `CarrierJourney` once unengaged and above `RETURN_RESERVE`.

### 6.2 The 1:1 Wall-Time Weapon Triad (`D-35-8`)

All combat inside a `LocalSpaceBubble` runs on **1:1 wall time**:

| Weapon Class | Effective Range | Kinematics (1:1 Wall Time) | Flight / Resolution Time | Active Counterplay |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Torpedoes** (`torpedo_tube`) | $10\text{ – }500\text{ km}$ | Inherits $\mathbf{v}_{\text{ship}}$; accelerates at $15\text{–}30\text{ g}$ ($150\text{–}300\text{ m/s}^2$) under 3D proportional navigation while $\Delta v > 0$ ($8\text{–}15\text{ km/s}$), then coasts ballistically up to `max_flight_ms` (`90_000 ms`) | **$12\text{ – }90\text{ real seconds}$** (tracked live across steps on the Holo-Tank) | Automatic **PDCs** (`+tac/pdc auto`), **Defensive Lasers**, target-locked **ECM/decoys**, or high-G burn |
| **Energy Weapons** (`laser_mount`, `pulsed_beam`) | $20\text{ – }500\text{ km}$ | Speed of light ($c$, instant hit) | **Instant per step**; builds hull heat cell (`heat_permille`) | Range diffraction ($1 / (1 + (R/R_0)^2)$), radiator management, attitude rolling across `front`/`left`/`right`/`rear`/`top` facets |
| **Railguns** (`railgun_spinal`, `railgun_turret`) | $10\text{ – }250\text{ km}$ | Unguided tungsten slug at world velocity $\mathbf{v}_{\text{slug}} = \mathbf{v}_{\text{ship}} + \mathbf{v}_{\text{muz}}$ aimed at world intercept $\mathbf{r}_{\text{target}} + \mathbf{v}_{\text{target}} \cdot t_{\text{tof}}$ | **$1.0\text{ – }15\text{ real seconds}$** time-of-flight | **Cannot be intercepted by PDCs or lasers.** Dodged by **lateral jinking thrust** during time-of-flight outside the shooter's predicted lead pip |
| **PDCs / CIWS** (`pdc_rotary`) | $0\text{ – }15\text{ km}$ (anti-ship) / terminal envelope (anti-torpedo) | $3\text{ km/s}$ rotary bursts with per-mount cooldown (`cooldown_ms`) and deterministic hit accumulation | Automatic terminal intercept pass when toggled on (`+tac/pdc auto`) | Saturation salvos that exceed PDC fire-rate capacity; stripping PDC mounts with lasers; magazine depletion (`W15`) |

### 6.3 Hull Sections, Facets & Subsystem Crits (`D-35-1`)

Rather than inventing a second damage or hull schema, space combat resolves hits directly against the hull's canonical unit definition (`dray.json`, `dray_p.json`, `halcyon.json`, etc.):
- **Facet Bands**: Incoming bearing in the ship's local frame selects `front`, `left`, `right`, `rear`, or `top` armour facets; rolling the ship (`+helm/roll` or `q`/`e`) rotates fresh armour facets toward the threat.
- **Sections (`hull`, `hold`, `drive` on `Dray`; berth/hull sections on `Halcyon`) & Components**:
  - **Radiator / Heat Cell Crit**: Lowers thermal dissipation per step, choking laser fire rate and raising IR signature.
  - **Drive Crit**: Degrades `accel_mg` or leaks propellant.
  - **Sensor / PDC Crit**: Degrades contact locks or opens a gap in the automatic PDC defense arc.

## 7. Zero-G Asteroid Field Mining & Registry Claims (`D-35-13`)

### 7.1 Orbital Asteroid Sites (`doc 20 §17` + 3D Bubble)

Four orbital asteroid clusters are authored:
1. **`ceres_halo`** — Inner Ceres co-orbital rubble cloud ($2.77\text{ AU}$, Core zone; Freeport Registry commons).
2. **`kirkwood_gap_3_1`** — 3:1 Jupiter resonance scatter at **$2.50\text{ AU}$** ($P \approx 1443.8\text{ days}$, Frontier/Contested; patrolled by the Kirkwood Customs Picket and raiders).
3. **`pallas_inclined_belt`** — High-inclination ($i = 34.8^\circ$) cluster near Pallas (Frontier; contested Pallas Syndicate paper claims).
4. **`saturn_b_ring_edge`** — Outer-system shepherd cluster near Titan/Saturn (Frontier; deep-space expedition site).

### 7.2 Spectral Classes $\rightarrow$ Canonical Ore Families & `stow.ore`

Each cluster contains seeded, slowly drifting asteroid pockets backed by `OreDeposit` pools (`doc 20 §3.4`) with richness grades `barren`, `lean`, `fair`, and `rich`:
- **C-Type (Carbonaceous Chondrite)** $\rightarrow$ `ice_ore` ($1000\text{ L/t}$) and `carbon_ore` ($1200\text{ L/t}$).
- **S-Type (Stony-Metallic)** $\rightarrow$ `metal_ore` ($500\text{ L/t}$).
- **M-Type (Nickel-Iron / Platinum Core)** $\rightarrow$ rich `metal_ore` ($500\text{ L/t}$) and `strategic_ore` ($600\text{ L/t}$).

All mined matter enters **`stow.ore`** (measured in `kg` and `L` using `goods.json`'s canonical specific volumes), requiring an ore-capable hull (`Dray-P`'s $86{,}000\text{ kg}$ hold or drone/TV hoppers) and respecting `hold_unreserved` (`D-20-9`).

### 7.3 The Zero-G Mining Loop & Registry Consequences

1. **Rights & Claims**: Just as on surface sites (`D-20-11`, `D-20-12`), scanning (`+mine/ping`) and flying are free, while extraction and claim-staking consult the **Freeport Registry** (`port/licence.rs` prospecting licence at 2500 cr / 168 IC days, block/rock stake via `surface/claims.rs`, and **30 IC-day** claim contest window via `surface/tenure.rs:34`). Mining an unleased or Syndicate-claimed rock without paper works in frontier space but spikes thermal bloom and records an actionable claim-jump event.
2. **Velocity Match (`+helm/match <rock_id>`)**: The pilot flies within **$2{,}000\text{ m}$** of the target asteroid pocket and nulls 3D Euclidean relative speed to **$|\Delta \mathbf{v}| < 5\text{ m/s}$** (`5,000 mm/s`).
3. **Extraction (`+mine/laser <rock_id>` or `+mine/drones launch`)**:
   - **Ship Mining Laser**: Direct extraction over a timed run; generates high **thermal/IR bloom** visible across the cluster bubble and risks volatile outgassing $\Delta \mathbf{v}$ kicks if overheated.
   - **Collector Drones (`doc 20 §17`)**: Up to **4 drones per `Dray-P`** (`maximum_items = 4`), launched and sealed from bay to bay under a real `ExcursionId`, running the stock `mining` doctrine (`scan()`, `sound()`, `goto()`, `mine()`, `recover()`). **As built (doc 44 unit MW.12, Q-44-27):** each drone's sortie is a small bubble of its own that its mind flies with the intents every space mind has (`lock`, `helm`, `speed`, `hold`, and `fire` on its collector, which lifts a tonne from the rock it has locked). It brings back what it lifted and carried home, and its record leaves the excursion book when it is back aboard. The house doctrine is `drone_mining`, the fourth actor of the space cast; the surface verbs named above are not a space mind's.

## 11. The link-loss backup mind (Q-35d-2, ruled 2026-11-11; amends `D-35-7`)

The owner ruled on 2026-11-11 that a unit whose whole crew has lost its link is taken home by a mind, in the field and in space alike, and the same day ruled four points more as doc 44's Q-44-35 to Q-44-38 (doc 44 was PR #530, not on main, when this was written; this cut is its unit MW.8). This section is those rulings as rules, the answers to what they left open, and the plan for the half that is not built. The space half is built (cut notes, `SF.R32`); the field half is built too (§11.4, doc 44 cut `MW.7`). The same rules amend doc 27's Q-H5 and pin P-2.

### 11.1 Rules

- **B-1, the handover.** A unit passes to its backup mind when every crew member aboard has been without a link for five real minutes (`SPACE_LINKDEAD_GRACE_WALL_S`, 300). Inside the five minutes nothing changes from `D-35-7`: the drive is cut, point defence is held on AUTO. The mind keeps the helm until a crew member's link comes back (B-7) or the unit is alongside. A crew that leaves by quitting, with no lost link, hands over at once (Q-35-B1).
  - **Crew (Q-44-35)** are the characters aboard who hold one of her bridge stations, and her captain when aboard (`helm_crew_aboard`). A passenger is not crew: their link neither holds the mind off nor takes the helm back.
  - **The five minutes (Q-44-36)** start when the last crew member loses their link. An earlier crew member's own disconnect, when the server ends their session, does not shorten them.
- **B-2, one mind per unit.** The backup mind is a Rhai doctrine, thought once a space tick while there is something to decide. A hull on a corridor between ports is not thought for: she is already going where she filed.
- **B-3, which mind.** In this order, the first whose source the store holds and which thinks without a fault:
  1. the mind the player sealed on the unit: since doc 44 unit MW.13 a filed mind sealed with `+ship/mind` under the Roll's claim, thought with only while it still fits her core and her holder (`crates/tacsim/src/port/ship_mind.rs`). It may lock, which a house default may not; it never fires;
  2. the house default the store names: `tacsim:doctrine:linkdead_return`, a pointer (`{"sha256": …}`) to a `tacsim:mindsrc:` document;
  3. the house default compiled in, `content/minds/linkdead_return.rhai`, which every hull carries.
- **B-4, what it may and may not do.** The mind asks; a seat (`crates/tacsim/src/space/backup.rs`) acts, and the limits are the seat's, so no source can step past them.

  | May | Never |
  |---|---|
  | fly a passage to a port | open fire first |
  | pay for it from the unit's own tank and locker, on the paths a pilot's passage uses | mine |
  | heave to and submit to a customs inspection | trade, or draw from any vault but its own hold for its own passage |
  | hold where it is | run a customs picket under power |
  | keep point defence on AUTO, set its emission posture | fire at any unit that has not fired on it since the bubble opened (Q-44-38). This cut refuses every lock, shot and arming: Q-35-B3 |

- **B-5, a stop.** Inside a bubble the mind reads the stop on her from its view (B-8) and brakes her itself: a burn along the way the stopping ship moves against her, sized to end her drift in the step ahead, into her return reserve if it must. The seat adds no burn of its own (doc 44 unit MW.9; before it the seat worked the brake out on the mind's `hold()`). The one burn the seat lets through is one that takes her drift against the stopping ship off: she cannot run under power.
  - **Customs.** Once she is under the heave-to speed the picket reads her hove to. On the mind's `hold()` the seat then cuts her drive and submits her to inspection as her captain: the same ruling `+helm/submit` gets, fine or seizure included.
  - **An interdictor.** There is nothing to submit. Under the heave-to speed she is stood down, she alone, and is free to go (doc 44 Q-44-24).
- **B-6, where she goes.** Outside a bubble, on the mind's `speed(n)`, the seat engages the planner's passage (`engage_port_autopilot`: tank and locker checks, the debit) to, in order:
  1. the port filed as her destination on departure;
  2. the port she left, when she filed for a cluster or a body;
  3. every other open port, nearest first, taking the first she can afford;
  4. none in reach: she holds where she is and is a retrieval job (§11.3 Q-35-B6). The seat asks the planner again every 60 real seconds (`BACKUP_RETURN_RETRY_WALL_S`), because she drifts.
- **B-7, reconnect and restart (Q-44-37).** The step a crew member's link comes back, the mind stands down: its record is removed, any burn it ordered is cut, and the crew's first command is next. What the mind already did stands: a passage engaged stays engaged, a ruling filed stays filed. Nothing of the mind's is ever in flight across a step (a think and its acts are one step of the tick), so there is nothing else to discard. A restart while the mind has the helm resumes the mind: its record, memory included, is in `tacsim:space:state`.
- **B-8, what the mind reads.** Inside a bubble, the view every space mind reads (doc 44 unit MW.9; `crates/tacsim/src/space/view.rs`): her contacts as her sensors hold them, numbered for her alone in the order she first held them; through `space(v)` her own velocity, what her drive gives, her tank and return reserve, her emission posture, the stop on her (`none`, `hailed`, `hove_to`, `cleared`, `ran`) and the contact that holds it; through `space_contact(v, id)` a contact's elevation, velocity, posture and the stop it is under. `v.held` is true while a stop holds her. Outside a bubble: `v.motor.outcome == "no_route"` while the planner's last refusal stands. Nothing else of the world.

### 11.2 As built (space)

`SF.R32`. `SpaceLinkdeadState` carries the mind's memory, last trace word, last act and retry time. `step_backup_minds` (the space tick, outside bubbles) releases a hull that is alongside, leaves a corridor hull alone, and thinks for one adrift. `fly_in_bubble` (the bubble step) thinks, brakes and marks a hull for submission; `settle_bubble_backup` submits her. The one-shot return at the grace's end is gone.

Since doc 44 unit MW.9 `fly_in_bubble` no longer brakes: it thinks on the shared view and hands the mind's intents to the one applier (`crates/tacsim/src/space/applier.rs`), seated `Seat::ABSENT`, which drops every lock and shot and every burn but one that heaves her to. The house default's brake is in `linkdead_return.rhai`.

### 11.4 The field half (as built, doc 44 cut `MW.7`)

- **Before.** A field pilot is bound into the same `ArenaRegistry` as an arena pilot (`field/boot.rs`), so the arena's presence hook is the field's (`arena_bridge.rs`, `SeatFacts`). On a lost link the seat went `ControlState::Linkdead`; when the session ended, after the server's 300 s grace or on a quit, the seat was `Released` and the unit went to its sealed mind. The kernel seeds the Standard Doctrine `Withdraw` at a release, so the unit did drive for its party's home, but it rallied after a lull and fought, a sealed script was told nothing, nothing put the unit aboard, and a pilot who came back after the grace could not take it back.
- **Base** is the party's home: the cell at its carrier's ramp (`field/home.rs`, `homes`). A unit with no reachable ramp has no base and holds (Q-CF-18, Q-H3, Q-44-42).
- **`FB.1`, the record.** `Away { why, since_wall_s, handed }` on the unit's row of the field's live record (`field/absent.rs`, `field/record.rs`), opened by the seat's `Linkdead` hook, cleared when the pilot is back, kept across a restart. The five minutes are the field's own (`FIELD_LINKDEAD_GRACE_WALL_S`, 300 wall seconds, checked once a world second), not the server's session grace: a session that ends on a lost link no longer releases the seat.
- **`FB.2`, the hand-over.** `field::absent::hand_over` is the one door. At five minutes, at once on a clean QUIT (Q-44-46) and at once for a pilot incapacitated while connected (Q-44-43), the host tells the arena the unit is flown for an absent player (`ArenaMsg::Absent`), releases the seat, and tells the pilot and those the unit was with. The arena lays a standing `Withdraw` with no deadline on the unit's mind row, so the stock mind drives home and does not rally, and a sealed script reads the order as `v.order`. The host asks the unit out at its carrier's ramp once a world second (`home::ask_out`, which now has a rig's exit ask) and, since no mind may board, runs its recover as its pilot when the kernel lets it out, trying again every ten wall seconds until the unit is off the ground. A pilot who reconnects while the field runs is bound and seated again (`take_back`): the seat's `Live` control drops the mind's pending think and its order, and the task recalls a ramp exit it has not applied. A restart resumes the mind and seats only a pilot who is connected.
- **`FB.3`, the gate.** `arena::absent::permits` is the rule of Q-44-38, once: a shot only at a unit whose fire has arrived on this one, none at all while a hold the player left stands, no mining, no trading. The arena asks it in `Arena::resolve_fire_by`, where every shot no seat typed is resolved, so it binds the house default and a player's sealed mind alike. The facts are the unit's own (`UnitState::absent`, `UnitState::fired_on_by`, the seat's weapons hold or a standing order's `hold_fire`). A mind flying for an absent player is shown as hostile only the contacts it may answer. Space calls the same function with facts of its own; what it must provide is in `44-cut-notes.md`, `MW.7`.
- **`FB.4`, not built.** No ramp in reach is the retrieval job of Q-35-B6. Today the unit holds.
- **The escort that leaves** (the open point). It goes, and the lead is told: the leader of its own party and the leaders of the parties that stand its friends hear one line, queued for one who is offline.
- **Defaults taken and what is left** are in `44-cut-notes.md`, `MW.7`. Two are the owner's call: a seat left at weapons hold stays held, so such a unit answers nobody; and a sealed script that ignores the order is not driven home by the engine (Q-35-B4's default, taken for the field).

## 12. The cast's doctrines, by digest (doc 44 unit MW.10)

Q-44-25 as corrected in doc 44 §4a: a changed doctrine is a new revision under a new digest, published and selected, never an edit of stored bytes. Cut notes: `44-cut-notes.md`, MW.10.

### 12.1 Rules

- **C-1, the cast names a digest.** Each actor in `content/space/picket_cast.json` (`corvette`, `raider`, `interdictor`) names its doctrine and `doctrine_sha256`, the SHA-256 of that doctrine's source. A unit test holds each digest to the source the tree ships, and prints the value to write when a doctrine's bytes change.
- **C-2, the store holds the source.** The boot writes each shipped space doctrine as a `tacsim:mindsrc:<sha256>` document, once, as it writes Standard Doctrine. A key that is already claimed is never written again, readable or not.
- **C-3, which doctrine an actor's next seal names.** Staff's selection if the world holds one (`tacsim:doctrine:cast.<actor>`, a pointer `{"sha256": …}`), else the cast's. In both cases only if the store holds a readable source under the digest. A selection whose document does not read is not "no selection": the actor is refused until staff select again or clear it.
- **C-4, no doctrine, no sail.** An actor whose doctrine the store does not hold is refused: the boot logs one error line that names the actor and the digest and keeps it (`TacsimStore::cast_faults`), `@mind/cast` shows it, and every seal site opens no bubble for that actor, so a carrier crosses her waypoint unstopped. The doctrines are resolved before the carrier is checked out into a bubble, so a refusal leaves her as she was. The boot itself goes on, as it does past any document it cannot use. At a seal nothing falls back: not a selection to the cast's doctrine, and not the cast's to the bytes compiled into the binary. (A bubble that is already open thinks on by its seal's digest, and `source_lookup` may answer that digest from the binary if its document is lost; those are the same bytes.)
- **C-5, a seal is taken when the bubble opens.** A selection changes the next seal. An open bubble keeps its seal to its end; across a restart it comes back from the digest in the bubble's own record, and from the hull's `tacsim:mind_seal` attribute when that is the same seal, never from the cast.
- **C-6, staff select with `@mind/cast`.** `@mind/cast` lists the three actors. `@mind/cast <actor>=<owned mind>@<revision>` selects a revision the asker saved in the workshop; `=<64-character digest>` selects any source the store holds; `=PACK` clears the selection. The verb refuses a digest the store does not hold and a source that does not compile as a mind. It needs `SIM_ADMIN` or Royalty.
- **C-7, a closed bubble says what flew it.** `CompletedInterdictionBubble::flown` lists each NPC actor's doctrine name and digest. The record keeps no input tape (it is pruned at close), so this is where an earlier bubble says which doctrine it flew.

### 12.2 What a replay reads

`BubbleInputTape::replay` re-applies the commands on the tape to its first snapshot. It runs no think and reads neither the store nor the cast, so a replay of an open bubble comes out as the bubble did whatever has been selected since. The commands on the tape are what the sealed mind's intents became (`applier.rs`); which mind that was is the seal in the bubble's record (C-5) and, once it closes, `flown` (C-7).

### 12.3 Where the selection lives, and what was rejected

The selection is a document of the world (`tacsim:doctrine:cast.<actor>`), beside the store's sources and in the shape of §11's house-default pointer. It survives a restart, because the boot reads it, and it survives a content upgrade, because it is not content: after an upgrade `@mind/cast` shows the selected digest and the one the cast now names. Rejected: a field in the cast file (staff cannot change content on a running server, and an upgrade would overwrite it); an attribute on the NPC hull or its commander (those objects are made when a bubble first opens and are not there for the boot to check).
