## 3. SM.12a — the mill

### 3.1 Goal and acceptance

Each port-tick fire, after the intake drip, the Ceres stock clerk may submit one **mill batch**: when a house's storefront holds at least `batch_t` of an ore it mills and the house has milled less than `per_ic_day_t` of that ore this IC day, one authoritative bundle (`tacsim_port_mill`) debits `batch_t` of ore, credits `batch_t / ratio.ore × ratio.product` of the product into the same storefront, and advances that mill's ledger exactly once per batch ordinal. On a later fire the clerk **restocks** the house's standing ask through `mush_market::restock_ask` by exactly the milled units not yet listed, and the port map's per-mill counter rises by the same units in the same handler outbox (the `bid_step` precedent, `post_wanted` then `put_port_map`). Lane v14 authors the Exchange's `metal_ore` → `metals` and Occator's `ice_ore` → `volatiles`, both 5 : 2, 20 t a batch, at most 40 t of ore an IC day. Staff read every mill on `@tacsim/port status`; players see the ask grow and one sentence of `help trade`. The market still never mints.

**The §13 acceptance made concrete**: `tests/mill.rs::sm12a_a_month_of_milling_conserves_and_restocks_only_by_the_mill` drives the production composition root (the `plumbing.rs` rig) through 30 IC days of a fixed schedule of ore sales into both bids and purchases from the Exchange's ask, with one reboot mid-month between a batch and its restock, and asserts per day. **Its clock strides**: `plumbing.rs`'s `fire` is one `tw.set_now` step (`:462-464`), and a month fired densely is about 21,600 clerk fires in one test (720 an IC day at rate 24), and **the month runs at world rate 24**, not the rig's rate 1, because the programme's dormancy clock is real time (`dormancy_real_days` 7, `ceres_phobos.json:2586`; `port/recall.rs:819-826`) and a 30-IC-day month at rate 1 would recall the selling hulls at real day 7 (corrected in Draft 0.3), so the test fires densely only where an act can be due — after each scripted sale and purchase until the clerk reads Idle, around every IC-day boundary (the drip and the day tally), and around the reboot — and otherwise steps the clock in IC-hour strides; the month is then about 720–2,000 fires, and the pure twin in `port/mill.rs` carries the per-fire arithmetic. Its debug budget on the box is ≤ 90 s at `-j 8` (the claims suite's figure, `tests/claims.rs` 68.60 s), reported under COMMANDS. It asserts per day that the Exchange's `metals` listing moved by exactly (Δ ledger `product_t` − metals bought) and the day tally never passed 40 t; at the end, per mill, `ore_t × 2 == product_t × 5`, (Σ ore sold − the storefront ore pool) `== ore_t`, the listing `== 4,000 − Σ settled purchases net of refunds + product_t` (Occator `9,600 + product_t`), the product pool `== listing + undrawn`, no drift page, no Flagged Listings line, no account balance moved on any mill or restock fire.
