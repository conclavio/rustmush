## 1. Critical Findings (`C-01` – `C-21`)

### 1.1 Doc 48 — Core Exchange, Money, Services & Production (11)

#### `C-01` — Partial quote on an exact-lot ask steals buyer credits and permanently traps the seller's lot
- **Scope**: `EX.1a`, `Q-48-49`, `Q-48-50`, `48a A-3`
- **Locations**: `crates/mush-market/src/commands.rs:707-711` (`quote`), `crates/mush-market/src/trade.rs:167` (`is_current`), `crates/mush-market/src/trade.rs:355-358` (`quote`), `crates/mush-market/src/seller.rs:973` (`ListingAcceptance::stands`), `crates/mush-market/src/trade.rs:1200-1203` (`commit_quantity`), `crates/mush-market/src/trade.rs:1389-1397` (`purchase_spec`), `crates/mush-market/src/lot.rs:911-917` (`LotOp::Consume`)
- **Failure trace**: `+market/quote <id>` defaults an omitted `=<qty>` to `1`. `quote`, `is_current`, and `ListingAcceptance::stands` check only `quantity <= listing.quantity` instead of `quantity == listing.quantity` for `Supply::Lot { lot: Some(_) }`. On acceptance of a 10 t exact lot at qty 1, `commit_quantity` deletes the listing immediately; `LotOp::Consume` aborts (`moved.quantity() != quantity`); 30 s later the `Before`-only contract settles anyway. The buyer pays the seller + venue cut + levy for 0 goods, and the seller's 10 t lot stays permanently locked under `lot_holds[lot] = listing/<id>` (unwithdrawable because the listing is deleted).
- **Recommended fix**: Require `quantity == listing.quantity` when `listing.source.exact_lot().is_some()` in `quote`, `is_current`, and `ListingAcceptance::stands`; default `+market/quote <id>` to `listing.quantity` for exact-lot asks; bind `PaidKind::Hold` settlement to a delivery fact (`C-02`).

#### `C-02` — All exact-lot (`PaidKind::Hold` / `LotOp::Consume`) sales settle and pay the seller even when `LotOp::Consume` fails or is dropped
- **Scope**: `EX.1a`, `EX.3` (V7 parts), `48a A-3`, Doc 48 §4.3 Consume defect
- **Locations**: `crates/mush-market/src/trade.rs:664-1016` (`accept_at`), `crates/mush-market/src/trade.rs:1389-1397` (`purchase_spec`), `crates/mush-market/src/lot.rs:907` (`room_for`), `crates/tacsim/src/port/pools.rs:2076`, `crates/tacsim/src/port/executor.rs:2425-2457`
- **Failure trace**: Unlike `PaidKind::Draw`, `purchase_spec` emits only `TypedCondition::Before` for `PaidKind::Hold` (no `TypedCondition::Fact`, no `restage_consume`), and `accept_at` never checks `ctx.authoritative_pending(ctx.actor)` or `ctx.authoritative_queue_full()` (contrast `sell_into_bid` at `trade.rs:2554-2563`). Any rejected or dropped `Consume` (concurrent capacity race in `room_for`, pending authoritative action, full queue, or `C-01`) deletes the listing, settles the contract in 30 s, transfers buyer funds to seller + venue/levy, delivers 0 goods, and orphans `lot_holds` forever. Also affects PL.5/V7 part-lot buys and EX.1b offline personal-ask buys.
- **Recommended fix**: Emit a durable transfer/consume fact marker from `LotOp::Consume` and bind `PaidKind::Hold` contracts to `TypedCondition::Fact` + `Expiry::Refund`; pre-check `authoritative_pending` and `authoritative_queue_full` in `accept_at`; add `restage_consume` and release/relist the lot on refund.

#### `C-03` — Player-open `@inv/move` and `@inv/crystallize` plus systemic divisible `lot_tally` omission break locality, capacity, Co-op share, locker fees, departure mass, and hull-empty checks
- **Scope**: `EX.1a`, `EX.1b`, `EX.2`, `EX.2b`, `EX.4`, `Q-48-49`, `Q-48-52`, `Q-48-56`, `D11`
- **Locations**: `crates/mush-inventory/src/commands.rs:229-255,435-449,781-894`, `crates/mush-inventory/src/provider.rs:435-440,557-595,708-722,809-848`, `crates/mush-inventory/src/pool.rs:534,1172,1478`, `crates/mush-market/src/lot.rs:1125-1138,1769-1784`, `crates/tacsim/src/port_tick.rs:2630,2731`, `crates/tacsim/src/port/burn.rs:438-440`, `crates/tacsim/src/port/locker.rs:234-250`, `crates/tacsim/src/port/hull_sale.rs:171-177,271-277`, `crates/tacsim/src/port/works.rs:2682-2684`
- **Exploit trace**:
  1. `@inv/move` and `@inv/crystallize` have no `Role::Royalty` restriction. `InventoryVerb::Move` lists all `live_endpoints(world, actor)` across the entire solar system, attaches `capacity` only for `Dissolve` (`provider.rs:708`), and executes `families.transfer` with zero co-location, capacity, chartered-hold, or sale-lock checks. Any player can teleport bulk cargo instantaneously from Ceres to Titan (`@inv/move <ceres_vault>/<good>:<n>=<titan_locker>`), move Co-op chartered cargo into a locker to pay 0% Co-op share, overfill 20 t lockers and 80 t holds arbitrarily, or stuff cargo into a listed hull after `HullSale::admit`.
  2. Compounding this, every port vendor's seeded stock carries a `PoolTranche` that transfers to buyers, so players can `@inv/crystallize` bulk cargo (or simply buy any divisible personal exact-lot ask). Although `mush-inventory` maintains `lot_tally` for all commodities, five independent readers (`lot.rs:1131,1776`, `port_tick.rs:2630,2731`, `burn.rs:438`, `works.rs:2682`, `provider.rs:435`) early-return `pooled` whenever `!is_indivisible`/`!is_part`. Divisible exact lots therefore consume 0 capacity, incur 0 cr/IC-day locker rent (`locker::held_kg`), add 0 kg departure mass / 0 fuel burn, and pass `HULL_HOLD_LADEN` on EX.2/EX.2b hull sales.
- **Recommended fix**: Gate player `InventoryVerb::Move` through a composition transfer policy enforcing port co-location, destination `PoolCapacity`, chartered-hold restrictions, and sale locks (or restrict `@inv/move` / `@inv/crystallize` to staff if not player verbs); unconditionally add `lot_tally` to `pooled` across all five readers.

#### `C-04` — Cross-boundary `EX.1a × EX.1b × EX.10`: Player exact-lot undercuts permanently strand cargo in NPC haulers and drain Vesta Carrying Company's 400,000 cr capital
- **Scope**: `EX.1a`, `EX.1b`, `EX.10`, `Q-48-20`, `Q-48-56`
- **Locations**: `crates/tacsim/src/port/hauler.rs:521,664-671,736`, `crates/tacsim/src/port/executor.rs:657-725` (`ask_for_order`, `personal_ask`)
- **Exploit trace**: While `hauler::plan` passes `max_major: None` for consumable `Qty::Fill` orders (`hauler.rs:672-679`), its cargo `What::Buy` sets `max_major: Some(choice.ask_major.max(1))` (`hauler.rs:664-671`). That causes `ask_for_order` (`executor.rs:666-673`) to pick cheaper player personal exact-lot asks over the port house's ask. Purchased bulk exact lots land in `VCC-01`/`VCC-02`'s hold as `inventory.lots` (not `inventory.pools`). At the destination, `sell_into_bid` reads only `inventory.pools`, and `carry_trip` (`hauler.rs:521`) / `sell_what_is_aboard` (`hauler.rs:736`) call `read_pool` (which returns `0` for non-part lots). The cargo sits permanently stranded in the NPC hauler's hold at 0 apparent mass while the player repeats the undercut until Vesta Carrying Company's 400,000 cr capital is drained.
- **Recommended fix**: Fix `C-03` (`read_pool` + auto-dissolve or pool-credit on buy), and restrict NPC hauler `What::Buy` orders to house asks (or dissolve bought exact lots before departure/sale).

#### `C-05` — Every indivisible-good / part sale into a bid (`PaidKind::Deliver` + `lot: Some`) is silently wiped from `MarketStore` on reboot
- **Scope**: `EX.1a`, `EX.3b` (V10 scrap parts), `PL.8`
- **Locations**: `crates/mush-market/src/trade.rs:2626-2638,2780-2790` (`sell_into_bid`), `crates/mush-market/src/record.rs:1224-1228` (`PaidLot::check`)
- **Failure trace**: For indivisible goods (`sells_as_lots`), `sell_into_bid` writes `PaidLot { kind: PaidKind::Deliver, lot: Some(sold_lot), ... }`. On reboot, `store.rs` validates quotes through `PaidLot::check`, which rejects `self.lot.is_some() && self.kind != PaidKind::Hold` and discards the quote. Every server restart silently drops all in-flight part bid sales (breaking `restage_deliver`, settlement taping, `proceeds.settled`, and bid restoration) and wipes settled V10/PL.8/P2P part trades from the tape.
- **Recommended fix**: Permit `self.lot.is_some()` for `PaidKind::Hold | PaidKind::Deliver` in `PaidLot::check` (`record.rs:1224`), and add a boot-reload persistence test for indivisible bid sales.

#### `C-06` — Zero-cost permanent griefing lockout on ANY player hull via an unaccepted `@contract/offer`
- **Scope**: `EX.2`, `Q-48-52`, regression of `48b W-48-8.1`
- **Locations**: `crates/mush-market/src/trade.rs:1582-1590,1615` (`sells_title_of`, `asset_on_sale`), `crates/mush-economy/src/contract.rs:2129` (`check_offerable`), `crates/mush-economy/src/contract_live.rs:853-862` (`build_deliver`), `crates/mush-economy/src/commands.rs:1004` (`asset_target`), `crates/tacsim/src/port/bay_work/mod.rs:145,1322`, `crates/tacsim/src/port/bay_work/strip.rs:76`
- **Exploit trace**: Unlike `house_sale::is_sale_of` (`crates/tacsim/src/port/house_sale.rs:83-92`), `sells_title_of` checks only `!Draft && !is_terminal()` and `Deliverable::TitleTransfer { object }`, without verifying `from == title_owner` or `c.consent.accepted_by(from)`. Meanwhile `check_offerable` has an empty `Deliverable::TitleTransfer { .. } => {}` arm and `asset_target` resolves any visible object. An attacker drafts a contract with attacker + alt naming `TitleTransfer { object: victim_hull, from: attacker, to: alt }` with no deadline and calls `@contract/offer`. The victim cannot see or cancel the contract, and `asset_on_sale(w, victim_hull)` permanently blocks `+market/post` (`ALREADY_FOR_SALE`) and `+bay/strip`, `+bay/scrap`, `+bay/fit` (`UNDER_SALE`).
- **Recommended fix**: Require `from == current_title_owner` and `c.consent.accepted_by(from)` in `sells_title_of` (matching `house_sale::is_sale_of`), and require `from == title_owner` in `check_offerable`.

#### `C-07` — `@facility/owner` allows unilateral forced ownership transfer onto ANY player or organization without consent
- **Scope**: `PL.9`, `EX.9`, `Q-48-26`, `Q-48-45`, `Q-48-53`
- **Locations**: `crates/mush-industry/src/commands.rs:983-1048` (`facility_cmd` `"owner"` switch), `crates/tacsim/src/port/upkeep.rs:574-666`, `crates/tacsim/src/port/facility_sale.rs:243`
- **Exploit trace**: Despite `48-cut-notes.md:4125` describing `@facility/owner` as `(staff)`, lines `997-1001` permit any non-staff actor satisfying `mush_economy::acts_for(ctx.w, ctx.actor, facility.owner())` to reassign the facility to any character or organization without consent. A facility owner can run up EX.9 running-cost debt and mothballing, then execute `@facility/owner #<room>=<victim>`. Next IC day, `upkeep.rs:574-666` drains the seller's entire `debt_minor` and ongoing daily running costs out of the victim's account. Worse, `@facility/owner` bypasses `busy`/`laden`/unsellable checks, leaves the E4 asset title and stores vault on the old owner (permanently bricking `facility_sale::admit` because `facility.owner() != seller`), and keeps `Holding::Authored` if run on an authored facility.
- **Recommended fix**: Gate `@facility/owner` strictly on `crate::store::is_staff(ctx.w, ctx.actor)` so player/org transfers occur exclusively through PL.9's asset sale.

#### `C-08` — Buying a yard (or ordering a hull at your own yard) permanently deadlocks on completion in an infinite retry/error loop
- **Scope**: `PL.9`, `PL.5`, `PL.6`, `Q-48-26`, `Q-48-53`
- **Locations**: `crates/tacsim/src/port/works.rs:1157` (`book`), `crates/tacsim/src/port/works.rs:1320-1325` (`deliver`), `crates/tacsim/src/port/works.rs:1381` (`buy`), `crates/tacsim/src/port/works.rs:2204-2206` (`step`), `crates/tacsim/src/port/works.rs:2375-2393` (`house_collect`), `crates/tacsim/src/port/works.rs:2903-2908` (`WorksProvider::prepare_authoritative`)
- **Failure trace**: When a player or org buys Stickney or Ceres yard (PL.9) and orders a hull for themselves, `book()` sets `c.house = (&beneficiary == facility.owner()) == true`. That blocks `+yard/buy` (`:1381`) and restricts `+yard/deliver` to `stores(ctx, facility)` (`:1320`). Once materials are supplied and `WorkOrderState::Completed` is reached, `works::step` (`:2204`) automatically invokes `house_collect`, queuing `WorksAct { then: Then::Collected, lots: [kit_lot, ...] }`. `prepare_authoritative` (`:2903-2908`) unconditionally rejects moving a hull kit unless `owed.then == Then::Seized` (`"a bulk lot is pooled before it moves"`). `settle()` retries `MAX_TRIES = 4` times, clears `c.owed`, logs an error, and `step()` immediately calls `house_collect` again forever — preventing `+yard/collect` from ever commissioning the hull.
- **Recommended fix**: Never invoke `house_collect` for hull-kit orders (`c.front == Front::Yard` or `kit_lots(ctx, c).is_some()`); leave commissioning to `+yard/collect`. Gate `c.house` on `facility.holding().is_authored()` so owner-held facilities retain `+works/buy`, `+yard/buy`, and docked-hold delivery.

#### `C-09` — Facility sale ignores EX.9 unpaid running-cost debt and mothballing, dumping the seller's debt onto the buyer (`PL.9 ↔ EX.9` dropped handoff)
- **Scope**: `PL.9`, `EX.9`, `Q-48-45`, `Q-48-38`
- **Locations**: `crates/tacsim/src/port/facility_sale.rs:12-15,236-315` (`admit`, `state_digest`, `on_sold`), `crates/tacsim/src/port/upkeep.rs:154,536-667` (`PortUpkeep`, `running_costs`)
- **Failure trace**: PL.9 deferred debt checks/settlement to EX.9 (`48-cut-notes.md:4124`), and EX.9 never updated `facility_sale.rs` (comment `:13-15` still reads `"Running costs are not charged yet"`). `admit()` accepts indebted/mothballed facilities, `state_digest()` omits `UpkeepRow`, and `on_sold()` neither settles `debt_minor` nor clears `mothballed` nor updates `UpkeepRow.org`. Because `PortUpkeep.facilities` is keyed by room `ObjId`, next-day `upkeep::settle()` debits the seller's accumulated debt directly from the buyer's account, keeps the facility mothballed, and throttles the buyer's org.
- **Recommended fix**: Refuse listing/acceptance while `debt_minor > 0` in `facility_sale::admit` (or route `debt_minor` to `UPKEEP_SINK` first out of sale proceeds and refuse `price < debt_minor`); hash `debt_minor`/`mothballed` in `state_digest`; clear settled debt/mothballing and update `UpkeepRow.org` in `on_sold`.

#### `C-10` — `EX.6` Escort bond-farming exploit and impossible `field=ceres` posting
- **Scope**: `EX.6`, `Q-48-11`, `Q-48-13`, `Q-48-58`
- **Locations**: `crates/tacsim/src/services/escort.rs:555-567` (`watch`), `crates/tacsim/src/services/escort.rs:767-771` (`post_cmd`), `crates/tacsim/src/services/escort.rs:908-920` (`accept_cmd`), `crates/tacsim/src/services/mod.rs:120-126` (`entry_word`)
- **Exploit trace**: A malicious poster lists a high-fee escort (e.g., `fee = 100,000 cr`, requiring a `20,000 cr` 20% acceptor bond) and either (a) never departs / enters the field, or (b) passes `field=ceres` (`entry_word` accepts any port without checking `def.site.is_some()`, making field entry impossible). At `due_ic` (28 IC days), `watch()` marks the job failed (`WHY_FAR`, `:555-566`), and contract expiry runs `Expiry::Forfeit { to: fee_account }`, transferring the innocent escort's 20% bond to the poster with no escort cancel remedy.
- **Recommended fix**: Require `def.site.is_some()` for `Journey::Field` in `post_cmd`; if `started == false` at `due_ic`, cancel the contract via `cancel_job` (refunding both fee and escort bond) rather than forfeiting the escort's bond.

#### `C-11` — `EX.6` Escort passage tracking immediately fails legitimate escorts and forfeits their 20% bond
- **Scope**: `EX.6`, `Q-48-11`, `Q-48-46`
- **Locations**: `crates/tacsim/src/services/escort.rs:440-486` (`captured`, `journey_now`, `left`), `crates/tacsim/src/services/escort.rs:582-625` (`watch`)
- **Failure trace**: Four interacting defects break passage escorts (zero passage escort tests exist per `48-cut-notes.md:2043`):
  1. `left()` (`:481-483`) triggers `WHY_LEFT` whenever `docked_venue(w, escort) != Some(to)`. If the protected ship undocks from `from` even seconds before the escort ship (well within `TOGETHER_IC = 3,600`), the next port tick sees the escort docked at `from != to`, fails the job immediately, and forfeits the escort's 20% bond.
  2. `journey_now()` (`:447-457`) ignores `to` (`Journey::Passage { from, .. }`), ending the journey when `protected` docks at *any* port `!= from`.
  3. On the terminal tick where `ended == true` (`:593-612`), `protected` has already docked (`journeys.get(protected) == None`) or left the field, so `near()` returns `false` and the entire final tick `dt` is added to `journey_ic` with `near_ic += 0` (scoring 0% on short 1-tick sorties/passages).
  4. `captured()` (`:440-442`) checks only ground-field surrender (`field::raiders::yielded`), missing space capture/boarding.
- **Recommended fix**: Exempt `docked_venue(escort) == Some(from)` within `TOGETHER_IC` of departure in `left()`; check destination `to` in `journey_now()`; clamp or credit `dt` at arrival rather than counting post-arrival time as not-near; detect space capture/boarding in `captured()`.

### 1.3 Doc 49 — Space & Ground Combat Round Conservation (`A-14`) (5)

#### `C-13` — `+coop/refit` refills all space magazines, repairs all hull/armor damage, and clears space crits for free
- **Scope**: `A-14`, `CC.7`, `Q-49-12`
