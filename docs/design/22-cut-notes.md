# 22 — cut notes

One `## U.n` heading per cut of [22-unit-kit.md](22-unit-kit.md), in the order they land: what landed, what it deferred and why, how it was verified.

## U.0

The document (Draft 0.1, 2026-10-22), this file and the README row. The owner accepted the four proposed defaults (look separate from the rules pack, a 24x16 doll grid at most, four facings from `aspect` with eight reserved, the unit type with classification) and the build plan; asked afterwards whether the type should wait on a close reading as the chassis's section words do (16b cut Q.2), the owner chose the exact type at classification (D-22-5).

## U.1 and U.2

Built together (one commit), because the text doll and the team colours share the same renderers.

**U.1, the frame and team colours.**

- **The frame**: `arena::state::ChassisWords` (the per-unit table of chassis-static words, re-derived and never hashed) gains `unit_type`, the unit's definition id off `TacticalUnit::schema()`; `FrameContact` gains `unit_type: Option<SmolStr>`, set in `Arena::frame_scope` from the target's words **when the contact is classified** (`held.class.team()`), `None` before (D-22-5), and compared exactly by `FrameContact::same_as`; `Frame` gains the pilot's own `unit_type`. The `arena.cockpit` keyframe carries `team` and `unit_type` at the top (inserted after the big `json!` — the macro was at its recursion limit) and `unit_type` per contact. No frame version: fields added for the person in the seat are not one. The canonical fixtures are re-blessed (`cockpit_frame.json`, `view_frame.json`: BA and RB, classified, carry `tacsim.unit.line_tank`; X3 and X4 `null`).
- **Team colours**: `raycast::SpriteKind` is now `Red`, `Blue`, `Unknown`, `Wreck` (was `Hostile`, `Friendly`, …); `cockpit::contact_kind` reads the frame's `class` (the classified team), never `friendly`. Unknown is a neutral grey (was khaki), the lock's box and label a marker yellow (was hostile red). The text panel's contacts are coloured by team and a teammate is marked `friend`; the lock is yellow.

**U.2, looks and dolls.**

- **`mush_pixel::look`**: the look format (doc 22 §3) with its own check (`Look::parse`, which refuses unknown keys, an empty unit list, a doll over 24x16, ragged rows, a character the legend lacks, a legend character never drawn), `Doll::condition` (what is left of each part off the frame's `sections`: a named facet's armour, else the section's internals; destroyed is nothing) and `Doll::tones` (touching parts get different tones where they can, so two parts in the same state are told apart). `sprites` is accepted and unread until U.3.
- **Four shipped looks** (`crates/mush-pixel/looks/`): `tracked_tank.json` (the five line tanks, the heavy, the range target; a top-down grid with the hull's four facets as plates, the turret and barrel, the engine, the tracks), `wheeled_scout.json`, `biped.json` (upright, its left on the left: head, torso, arms, legs) and `drone.json` (a 10x5 body with its four facets). The haulers and the prospector are listed lookless with the reason (never in the arena); they get the section rows and no doll.
- **The text doll** (`cockpit_text::doll_rows`): the pilot's own look's grid in half-blocks, two grid rows to a text row (24x16 is 24 by 8), centred in the armour column, toned; replaces the hard-coded tank, so a biped pilot gets a doll.
- **The pixel doll** (`overlay::draw_doll`, the `Instruments` overlay): the same grid, a 3-pixel square a cell at scale, toned, each part outlined; a unit with no look gets a bar a section.
- **The contract test** (`mush-contract-tests/tests/unit_looks.rs`): every shipped unit type (fourteen, read from `crates/tacsim/content/units/`) has a look or is listed lookless with a reason; no unit is drawn by two looks; every unit a look names exists; every section of every unit it draws is in the doll; every doll part names a section, and a facet, each of its units has; every weapon short name is a weapon component of one of them.

**Verified.** `tacsim` lib (1,432) and `tests/cockpit.rs` (183; `i4_eight_watch…`, a load-sensitive test, failed once under the parallel run and passed alone), `mushd` `observer_rule` (32), `mush-bot` `arena_contract` (19), `mush-pixel`, `mush-net-ssh`, `mush-net-web`, `mush-ansi`, `mush-session` `resume`, the contract test, the web's vitest (655), clippy, rustdoc.

## U.3

The sprite sheets.

- **The format** (`mush_pixel::look::Sheet`, parsed by `Look::parse`): `size` (at most 64 a side), `size_cm`, `anchor`, a `palette` of one-character keys to **materials** (`team-dark`, `team`, `team-light`, `black`, `steel`, `pale` — a closed set, not free colours: see the budget below), `facings` (`front`, `rear`, `right` required, `left` optional; each a `still` frame, two to four `moving` frames and `muzzles` by weapon) and one to four `wreck` frames. Refused with a reason: a missing required facing, a row of the wrong width or a frame of the wrong height, a key the palette lacks, a material it does not know, a moving cycle outside two to four, an anchor or muzzle outside the frame, no wreck. `Sheet::frame` picks by facing (a missing left is the right mirrored), motion and step; `Sheet::wreck_frame` by facing.
- **Placeholder art** (`looks/tracked_tank.json` 32x16 at 7.2 m by 3 m: a side view with the barrel to the right, a front with the muzzle toward you, a rear with the engine grille, two-frame tread cycles, a wreck with the turret knocked askew; `looks/biped.json` 20x32 at 5 m by 10 m: front and rear with a four-frame walk, a side view with the gun arm forward and the legs scissoring, a wreck on its knees; `looks/unknown.json` 24x14: a grey hull with a question mark, the reserved unit id `unknown`). Drawn by a throwaway generator out of rectangles; the JSON is the source from here on.
- **The palette budget**: the raycaster's colours were 17 bases x 12 fog steps + 16 sky = 220. The three flat sprite bases (red, blue, unknown) are retired and the sprites get twelve **paints** — the three teams' dark, mid and light, steel, pale and the ground shadow — fogged in six steps (`SPRITE_FOG`): 14 x 12 + 16 + 12 x 6 = **256** exactly, pinned by a test. Black is the outline base, a wreck's body the old wreck base.
- **Drawing** (`raycast::draw_sheet`, behind the shared `draw_sprite`, so the raycaster and the heightfield producer both draw sheets): a `Sprite` carries an optional `SpritePose` (sheet, facing, moving, step); its box is the sheet's `size_cm` at its range with the anchor pixel standing on the ground under the contact; every pixel is hidden where the picture drew something nearer; a flat shadow ellipse about the anchor is drawn first over ground at about the unit's distance. The bracket rule is unchanged (no unit pixel shown, or under two pixels tall: the bracket, in its team's colour). `cockpit::contacts_as_sprites` gives each contact its look by `unit_type` (or the `unknown` look while unclassified), its facing from `aspect`, its motion from `moving`, and the picture's animation step (`CockpitPicture` counts draws; a step every three).
- **The contract test** also holds every sprite muzzle to a weapon component of the look's units, and the `unknown` look to drawing nothing else and having no doll.

**Measured** (release, the owner's box): the fixture cockpit at 640x256 with the HUD renders in 0.68 ms (0.65 before), PNG 4,060 bytes. `examples/fixture.rs` writes `sprites.png`, a line-up of the shipped sheets (a red tank side-on, a blue tank head-on, a blue biped striding toward you, a red biped walking left, an unknown, a wreck).

**Verified.** `mush-pixel` (the budget is 256; a sheet sprite is drawn in its team's colour, mirrored for its left face, with a shadow, a wreck without team colour; a sheet's check refuses what §3.2 says and picks its frames), `mush-net-ssh`, `mush-net-web`, the contract test, clippy, rustdoc. Not smoked live.

## Smoked live on dev2 (2026-10-22, at 34e4d24e)

Both halves rebuilt from origin/main (U.1–U.3 and G.1b on top of SM.10), full restart, kept store. Hand-played in w11term (kitty, 170x48) and Windows Terminal 1.24 over `ssh.exe` (forced sixel).

- **Team colours and sprites**: in a red-side match with the machines filling three a side, the two red teammates were identified at once and drawn **red** from the tank sheet, labelled `friend` in the contacts list, with red pips on the tape: RB head-on at 150 m ahead, RC side-on (the right frame, barrel forward) at 50 m when the look swung to it, RB's left face (the right frame mirrored) when it pulled ahead-left; shadows under each. Blue contacts arrived as `BB blue` and `X4 unknown` at 550–600 m.
- **The biped's doll**: fielding `biped_mk1` (`SIM UNIT`), the armour column drew the upright doll (head, arms, legs, toned) and its section rows; under fire the left arm went to 77/120 and the right arm grey at 0/120, as the bars said.
- **Windows Terminal**: the same screen over sixel at 640x232 shown 1700x616; the tank's top-down doll in half-blocks.

**What the smoke found, fixed at once (the commit after 34e4d24e).**

- **A far contact was invisible**: BB at 550 m dead ahead was drawn — three pixels fogged nearly to the horizon's colour — under the reticle's centre, so the bracket rule (which fired only when *nothing* was drawn) never fired. A contact drawn under 10 pixels tall, or in the last third of the fog, is now bracketed as well, at least 10 pixels a side in its unfogged team colour (`raycast::MARK_FAR_PX`).
- **The picture drew from the map's corner after the match**: a frame with `seated: false` carries no pose, and the camera fell back to 0,0. The cockpit picture now draws a plain `NOT IN A COCKPIT` card (`overlay::draw_card`), which also replaces the last picture on screen.
- **The biped's torso fell off the armour column**: eight doll rows and six sections are fourteen rows, one more than a 48-row terminal's panel holds. Where they do not fit, the sections now pair up two a row with shorter bars (`armour_rows`' `rows`).

## U.4

The web client's doll, from the same looks.

- **One source**: the web bundles `crates/mush-pixel/looks/*.json` at build time (`import.meta.glob` in `web/src/lib/look.ts`), so the cockpit tab's doll and the terminal's are the same file; the files are checked where they live (`Look::parse`, the `unit_looks` contract test) and read leniently here — a malformed look is no look. The legend's characters are taken in sorted order, as the Rust reader takes them, so a part's index and its tone agree on both sides.
- **The frame**: the web's decoder keeps the frame's own `unit_type` (`CockpitFrame.unit_type`) — the pilot's own, never a secret from them.
- **The doll** (`components/DollView.svelte`, at the top of the damage pane): the grid as SVG, one square a drawn cell, coloured by `dollColour` (the instruments' green, amber, red and grey) from `dollCondition` (a named facet's armour, else the section's internals, none once destroyed), touching parts in the same state a lighter shade apart (`dollTones`, the same greedy pick as `Doll::tones`); each square carries its section's name as a tooltip. A unit type with no look gets the damage rows alone, as before. The pane's rows and bars below it are unchanged.
- The cockpit screen's observer rule is untouched: the look is static art keyed by the frame's own field, not a lookup into another store.

**Verified.** vitest (672: every shipped look read and the same count as the directory, a broken doll refused, the sorted legend order, the fixture tank's facet and internals and destroyed track, the tones apart for touching hull, turret, plate and engine, the ramp, and the mounted screen drawing the 24x16 grid's 352 cells), `svelte-check` with no errors or warnings. Not smoked in a browser yet.


## U.5 (2026-10-27)

The art. The three sheets U.3 shipped as placeholders — the tank, the biped and the `unknown` look — redrawn, starting from what the pilot sees rather than from the JSON: every sheet was rendered through the raycaster at 100 m, 300 m and 1 km first (`examples/sheets.rs`, new: one 640x480 PNG a sheet a range, the poses side by side on grass), and the pass was judged on those renders. The PNG importer the U.5 row also names is deferred; the format is unchanged.

- **What the picture makes of a sheet.** A frame is nearest-sampled (the floor of each pixel's centre) to `size_cm` at the contact's range: in the 640-wide frame a pixel a `cm × 4.8 / range_m`, so the tank is 34x12 pixels at 100 m, 11x4 at 300 m and 3x1 at 1 km (bracketed), the biped 25x53, 8x18 and 2x5. One-pixel lines and outlines vanish or alias at the first downsample and the old art was mostly outline, which is why a tank at 300 m was a black smudge. The new sheets are drawn as **masses**: `team-light` on every upward face, `team` on the sides, `team-dark` on undersides and recesses, `black` only where the thing is black (tracks, guns, feet), `steel` for wheels, links and barrels, `pale` for the visor, the headlights, the `?` and the dust the moving frames kick up. No outlines.
- **The tank** (`looks/tracked_tank.json`): was 32x16 at 7.2 x 3 m, a box on a box with no barrel head-on and a one-pixel barrel side-on. Now **56x20 at 7 x 2.5 m** — the sim's side silhouette (`line_tank`: 700 x 250), 12.5 cm a pixel, anchor 28,19. Side-on: a turret set back of centre over a hull with a sloped glacis over a track run with eight steel road wheels; the barrel is two pixels thick and a third of the length, with a mantlet. Head-on and from behind the hull is the sim's 3.5 m (28 of the 56 pixels): two tracks seen end-on under fenders, the glacis between them with headlights and a driver's slit (front) or the engine grille and twin exhausts (rear), the turret with the gun's bore ring (front) or a stowage box (rear), a cupola. Moving: the links step along the top and bottom runs side-on and the stripes step down the track faces head-on, and pale dust kicks up behind, alternating. The wreck: the turret knocked askew with its barrel drooping, the track thrown off the rear wheels, the deck caved in at the engine, smoke. Muzzles: cannon and laser at the bore (front 27,6) and the barrel's end (right 55,5); missiles on the turret.
- **The biped** (`looks/biped.json`): was 20x32 at 5 x 10 m, a stick figure with a box for a gun. Now **24x50 at 5.2 x 11 m** — the sim's front silhouette (`biped_mk1`: 520 x 1100; the mk2's 540 x 1150 shares it), 22 cm a pixel, anchor 12,49. A mech: shoulders the frame's full width with pads, a chest plate, a small head with a pale visor, dark-edged torso and arms, legs with knees and black feet with steel toe plates; the gun arm's bore ring head-on, a three-pixel gun held out ahead side-on, a backpack with steel vents from behind (and the gun's stub). Side-on the unit is the sim's 3.4 m, hunched forward. The walk, four frames: head-on and from behind one foot lifts three pixels while the body bobs and the arms swing, and in the planted frames the other foot is already an inch up; side-on a four-pixel stride with the knee bent, passing frames between. The wreck lies on its side: one leg up, the other flat, the head off, the gun arm flung aside, smoke. Muzzles: cannon and laser at the bore (front 20,18) and the gun's end (right 23,14).
- **The `unknown` look** (`looks/unknown.json`): was a grey box on a tread line with a `?` — a tank's tell on a contact that is not yet anything. Now **32x16 at 7 x 3.5 m** (unchanged), anchor 16,15: a rounded hump, light on top and dark under, a pale `?` on it, no tread and no limb, the same from every side; moving is a one-pixel bob; the wreck a flattened dark hump with smoke and no `?`.
- **`size_cm` honesty.** A sheet carries one size for all its facings, so it is the widest silhouette (side-on for the tank, head-on for the biped) and the narrower facings draw the unit inside that box, centred on the anchor column; a head-on tank is 3.5 m wide in a 7 m box. The shadow ellipse is the renderer's and still spans the box.
- **The check** (`look::tests::every_shipped_sheet_stands_moves_and_wrecks`): `Look::parse` already refuses frames of the wrong size, a character the palette lacks, and an anchor or muzzle outside the frame; the new test holds the shipped sheets to what parse cannot know is art — every still frame stands on its anchor (a drawn pixel on the anchor's row or the one above, so the unit meets its shadow), every muzzle sits on a drawn pixel (on the weapon, not in the air beside it), no two frames of a moving cycle are the same, and no wreck is a facing's still. It found the biped's two planted frames identical on the first pass.
- Drawn by a throwaway generator out of rectangles, ellipses and a walk-cycle rule, as U.3's were; the JSON is the source from here on.
- **The renders** the pass was judged on are kept under [images/22-u5/](images/22-u5/): `<look>_before_after.png` (the raycaster's 100 m line-up before and after, then the new art at 300 m and 1 km) and `<look>_sheet.png` (each sheet magnified, then nearest-sampled to its 100 m and 300 m pixel sizes).

**Verified.** `cargo test -p mush-pixel` (80 lib tests with the new sheet check and the biped's new size in the frame-picking test; the overhead, proving-ground and web goldens — the web golden re-blessed with `PIXEL_BLESS=1`: of its six hashes only flat/look-0 changed, the locked tank's fogged pixels at 440 m, looked at before accepting), the `unit_looks` contract test (every muzzle still names a weapon component), `mush-net-ssh`'s `tui_golden` (25, unchanged: the text cockpit draws the doll, not the sheets), the web's wasm module rebuilt and vitest (754, the parity cases hashing the same as the native render), fmt, clippy and rustdoc with `-D warnings`. Not smoked live; nothing deployed.
