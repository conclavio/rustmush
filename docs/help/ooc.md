# In and out of character

The ports of the corridor are in character. Limbo, the OOC Area and the
simulator are out of it. No exit of a port leads out of character: two verbs
carry you across.

  +ooc             Step out of character. From any room of a port it takes
                   you to Limbo and remembers the room you left.
  +ic              Go back in. It puts you in the room you left with +ooc.

If the room you left is gone, or closed to you now (a locked office you no
longer hold the key to), `+ic` takes you to the arrivals of the port you last
stood in instead. With no port remembered it takes you to Ceres Freeport, where
the Limbo door `c` goes. A port you can only reach by ship has no door from
Limbo, so `+ic` is the way back to it.

`+ooc` is refused while you are in a simulator pod ("EXIT" leaves it), out in a
field, aboard a ship or another vehicle, on your way somewhere, or held in
custody. Leave those the way the story leaves them, then step out.
