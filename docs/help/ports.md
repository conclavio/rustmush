# Ports

The corridor has five ports. **Ceres Freeport** is the Belt's market: the
Co-op's Registry desk, the Exchange, the chandlery and the berths. **Stickney
Yards** sits on Phobos above Mars: the Yard builds hulls, and Kasei Metals buys
what the Belt ships in. **Aitken Highport** is the Concord's civil anchorage in
low polar orbit over Luna: the Belt's concentrate and ice come up to it, and
lunar alloy and the basin fabs' instrument boards leave it. **Voltaire
Anchorage** is the Republic's bonded anchorage on Deimos, over the Republic
Strategic Reserve: it buys what Mars takes from the Concord and from Titan, and
sells only reaction mass and provisions. **Ligeia Terminal** is the Titan
Concession Company's spar in low orbit over Titan: the Company sells carbon
billet, water ice and the cheapest reaction mass in the system, and the Selk
Townships buy alloy and boards at the frontier's price. Every port but Ceres is
reached only by ship.

## Getting in and back

From Limbo the door `c` takes you to Port Watch Arrivals at Ceres Freeport. The
game remembers the port you last stood in when you step out to Limbo or the OOC
area. A port with no door from Limbo is one you can only reach by ship: the door
will not take you while that port is remembered, and `+ic` puts you back where
you left the corridor — Stickney's berth gallery, Aitken's Orbital Access
Arrivals, Voltaire's berth gallery, or Ligeia's Company Arrivals.

## The lanes

A Dray sails only the lanes its port posts; `+ship/plan` with no port plans every
one your berth clears for. A port is named by its body (luna, deimos, titan) or
by the start of its name (aitken, voltaire, ligeia).

  Ceres Freeport       to Stickney Yards, by way of Kirkwood Depot, and to
                       Aitken Highport
  Stickney Yards       to Ceres Freeport
  Aitken Highport      to Voltaire Anchorage and to Ligeia Terminal
  Voltaire Anchorage   to Stickney Yards: the hop down to Phobos, a few IC hours
  Ligeia Terminal      to Aitken Highport and to Voltaire Anchorage

Some lanes are not posted, and the corridor knows why:

  Aitken to Ceres         nothing at Ceres bids for what Luna sells — Ceres
                          buys no boards, and Freeport Works takes its alloy
                          from Kasei — and a Co-op hull bound for Ceres calls at
                          the depot by charter, so the way home would be the
                          long one. The Belt's boards reach it by other carriers
                          and the importers' own agents.
  Stickney to Voltaire    the Republic does not bond its own yard stock.
  Ceres and Ligeia        the Company clears through Luna and posts its lanes
                          to Aitken and Voltaire; from the Belt the passage
                          would want a slower throttle still.

Titan's lanes fit a Dray's tank only throttled (`help trade`).

## Ceres Freeport

You walk it by its exit words. From Arrivals:

  registry      the Registry & Co-op desk, where you sign with the Co-op
  concourse     the Freeport Concourse, the public hall
  exchange      (from the concourse) the Freeport Exchange: metal concentrate
                and water ice for sale, and Freeport Works buying alloy
  chandlery     (from the Exchange) reaction mass, voyage provisions, and the
                Co-op buying the Yard's crated fittings
  arrivals      back to Arrivals from the desk or the concourse
  lobby         (from Arrivals) the Pressure Lobby
  gallery       (from the lobby) the Visiting Berth Gallery, where the Drays
                are cradled

## Aitken Highport

The highport is a spun drum. A Dray berths at its hub, which does not turn: you
arrive among the hub cradles in the Visiting Berth Gallery, weightless, and the
spoke ladder takes you down into the drum, which turns at the Moon's own sixth
of a gravity. Nothing in this trade lands on Luna itself. A Dray comes in from
Ceres or Ligeia and leaves for Voltaire or Ligeia; you board her in the gallery.

On the Hub Deck, from the gallery:

  lobby         the Pressure Lobby, at the foot of the spoke ladder
  dockmaster    (from the lobby) the Dockmaster's Office of Concord Orbital
                Access: the sailing board, and reaction mass on its board
  receiving     (from the office) Habitat Board Receiving, where the Lunar
                Habitat Board buys water ice and carbon billet and Cabeus Works
                buys concentrate
  drum          (from the lobby) the spoke ladder into the drum, to Orbital
                Access Arrivals

On the Drum Deck, from Arrivals:

  concourse     the Drum Concourse, the spun deck's one wide hall
  registry      the Registry Annex, the Registry's Luna desk, where the export
                permits on Crisium's crates are stamped
  clearing      (from the annex) the Clearing House Window: Concord currency
                clearing, and Crisium's boards on its board
  exchange      (from the concourse or the clearing house) the Cabeus Exchange:
                Cabeus Works' alloy stock, Crisium Instruments' instrument
                boards, and the Board's and Cabeus's bids
  down          (from the exchange) the stair to Habitat Board Receiving
  victualling   (from the concourse) the Victualling Office: reaction mass and
                voyage provisions
  bar           (from the exchange or the Victualling Office) the Cold Trap,
                where Tycho's underwriters drink
  hub           (from Arrivals) the spoke ladder back to the Pressure Lobby

Cabeus Works' sales agent and Crisium's export factor sign at the Exchange, the
Board's receiving commissioner at Receiving, and the Victualling Office's purser
at its counter.

## Voltaire Anchorage

The anchorage is dug into the floor of Voltaire crater on Deimos. Deimos holds
half of what Phobos does: a Dray comes in on a long, throttled fall from Ligeia
or a shorter one from Aitken and settles into a cradle with tie-downs, handlines run everywhere, and every bench
has a tether loop. You arrive in the Visiting Berth Gallery and board there. Its
one lane out is the hop down to Stickney.

From the gallery:

  lobby         the Pressure Lobby
  customs       (from the lobby) the Republic Customs Post
  concourse     (from the customs post) the Roads Concourse
  exchange      (from the concourse) the Bond Exchange, where Syrtis Bonded
                Imports buys lunar alloy and instrument boards
  down          (from the exchange) the Reserve Receiving Hall, where the
                Strategic Reserve buys water ice and carbon billet and Syrtis's
                bids take goods too
  commissary    (from the concourse) the Reserve Commissary: reaction mass and
                provisions from the Reserve's rotation sales
  roadsmaster   (from the concourse) the Roadsmaster's Office of the Voltaire
                Bond Authority, with reaction mass on its board
  moorings      (from the concourse) the Ark Moorings Gallery, the last ark
                hulk in its window
  bar           (from the concourse or the moorings gallery) the Long Straw

Concord and Company goods enter Mars here in bond and leave it only by the hop
to Stickney; Belt cargo clears at Stickney's own Customs Hall, as it always has.
The Reserve's quartermaster signs its sales and its bids, and Syrtis's bonded
buyer signs Syrtis's.

## Ligeia Terminal

The terminal is a long unspun spar in low orbit over Titan, weightless and cold;
the Company's lifters climb to it out of the haze from the sea it is named for.
A Dray reaches it only throttled, from Aitken, and leaves for Aitken or
Voltaire; you arrive in the Visiting Berth Gallery and board there. It is a
company town: the arrivals desk, the sales floor, the store and the book are the
Company's, and one counter is not — the Selk Townships'.

From the gallery:

  lobby         the Pressure Lobby
  arrivals      (from the lobby) Company Arrivals, the Lift Marshal's desk
  concourse     (from the lobby or Arrivals) the Spar Concourse
  dockmaster    (from the concourse) the Dockmaster's Office ("come slow or
                don't come"), with reaction mass on its board
  exchange      (from the concourse) the Company Sales Floor: the Titan
                Concession Company's carbon billet, water ice and reaction
                mass, and the Townships' bids
  down          (from the sales floor) Townships Receiving, where the Selk
                Townships Board buys alloy and instrument boards
  bunkering     (from Receiving) the Bunkering Yard: the Company's ice and
                reaction mass
  store         (from Arrivals) the Company Store: voyage provisions, at the
                Company's price
  lifter        (from the concourse or the sales floor) the Lifter Gallery and
                its long window
  bar           (from the concourse or the store) the Ninety-Four

The Company's sales clerk signs at the Sales Floor, the storekeeper of the
Concession Stores at the store, and the Townships' purchasing delegate at
Receiving. News comes by light, over an hour old from Luna and up to an hour and
a half, and the boards are priced for the two months a hull takes.

## The boards

Every trading room has a board, and `look` reads it. Each line is one house's
offer — the price, how much is left, the house and the clerk who signs for it —
or one house's bid: a wanted line, with the price it pays and how much more it
will take. `+market/list` in a port shows that port's listings, each with the
number you quote it by.

Stock is finite and every tonne of it is on record. A line counts down as it
sells; when a board says SOLD OUT, it is. A wanted line takes goods until its
intake is taken, and then the board reads it as not buying.

Money is credits, written like `5600.00cr`. `+credit` shows what is posted to
your account, what is held against deals you have accepted, and what you can
spend now.

See also: `help ships`, `help coop`, `help trade`, `help orders`.

## Berth and departure boards

`look` in a station's berth gallery shows the hulls currently alongside, their
registry marks, registered title and agent, and public clearance status. A private
registry stays unnamed. The berth field names the gallery; registry marks tell
hulls apart.

At the dockmaster or harbourmaster, `look` also shows filed departures and the
origin port's underway sailings. FILED is a scheduled departure awaiting clearance;
CLEARED has a frozen passage and arrival estimate; OVERDUE is a runnable departure
whose hour has passed. IDLE means no current public clearance. A captain's cargo
orders, prices, passengers and private holds never appear on these boards.
