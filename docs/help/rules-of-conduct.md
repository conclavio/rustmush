# Rules of Conduct

(Placeholder — each game running this server should replace this topic
with its own policy. It ships so "help rules-of-conduct" always answers.)

Until your staff writes the real thing, the short universal version:

1. Consent matters. Do not force scenes, themes, or contact on players
   who have said no — in character or out.
2. No harassment. @ignore exists and staff read logs when they must;
   evading someone's mute or ban is itself a violation.
3. Keep IC and OOC separate. In-character conflict is play; carrying it
   out-of-character (or retaliating IC for OOC grudges) is not.
4. One account per person unless staff says otherwise. Do not share
   passwords.
5. No cheating: exploiting bugs, abusing building quotas, spam, or
   automating play beyond what staff allows. Report exploits with
   +request.
6. Staff decisions can be appealed calmly through +request — not argued
   across public channels.

Contact staff: use +request, or page anyone on WHO marked as staff.
